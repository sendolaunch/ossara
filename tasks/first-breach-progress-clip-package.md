# First Breach Progress Clip Package

Use the normal mission camera. This package is for a short progress clip or human visual review, not final marketing footage.

## Capture Goal

Show that OSSARA has moved from prototype into a playable vertical slice:

- Ward Crystal defense has a clear objective and focal point.
- Build phase, Barricade/Spike-gate placement, wave combat, Warden abilities, and loot can all be shown in one route.
- First Breach now reads as ruined kingdom courtyard meets plague cathedral, while still being honest progress footage.
- Inventory / Forge, rewards, and map art are functional foundations, not final presentation art.

## Recommended Length

- Quick social clip: `20-35 seconds`.
- Internal review route: `60-90 seconds`.

For social, keep the edit mission-focused: shrine, build, wave start, Warden action, loot beat. For internal review, linger long enough on the map readability questions.

## Exact Capture Route

### Primary Route

1. Open `?devMission=first-breach`.
2. Hold on the opening view for `2-3s`, showing the Ward Crystal shrine, central stair route, and side breach silhouettes.
3. Press `1` and place `Warden Barricade` at the central stair/main choke.
4. Press `2` and place `Spike-gate` nearby so the high-health wall and damaging blockade read as different choices.
5. Briefly show the softened build/choke markers and Ward-green lane language.
6. Press `Enter` or click `Start Wave`.
7. Let Rotlings enter the central lane so the spawn gate, stairs, landing, and lane strip are visible in one shot.
8. Use left-click for the Warden basic sword attack.
9. Use `Q` for Ward Slam on a small cluster.
10. Use `Space` to Dash/reposition.

### Optional Loot Route

1. Open `?devMission=first-breach&devLoot=1`.
2. Use the dev-only chest/reward control for internal capture only, or open a mission chest if recording normal player flow.
3. Show the world drop beam/glow before pickup.
4. Move close enough to show the tooltip/compare panel.
5. Pick up the item and briefly show the reward log or Inventory / Forge update.
6. Keep the Inventory / Forge panel on screen only briefly.

## Shot List

- Ward Crystal shrine hero shot.
- Central stair / verticality shot.
- In-world Ward marker shot: floor seams, small glyph stones/gems, and softened choke markings.
- Build placement shot with `Warden Barricade`.
- Build placement shot with `Spike-gate`.
- Enemy approach shot with Rotlings entering the central lane.
- Warden basic combat shot.
- Ward Slam impact shot.
- Dash reposition shot.
- Loot beam / pickup shot.
- Optional victory panel shot: `FIRST BREACH CLEARED` / `The Ward holds.`

## What To Avoid Showing

- Do not linger on artificial choke rings too long.
- Avoid tight framing on any remaining temporary helper primitives; show them as part of the in-world Ward floor language instead.
- Do not frame unfinished edges, greybox seams, or debug-looking helper geometry as final art.
- Do not focus on Inventory / Forge art for long; it is functional/basic.
- Do not show dev-only reward controls in public-facing clips.
- Do not overclaim final art quality or release timing.
- Do not make the clip feel like a feature-complete launch trailer.

## Human Review Checklist

After recording, review the clip with these questions:

- Does the Ward Crystal look cool?
- Do the stairs and verticality read?
- Are lanes readable?
- Are enemies visible against the map?
- Are Barricade and Spike-gate visible?
- Does combat look fun?
- Does the map look too cluttered?
- Is it too dark?
- Are the green rings helpful or ugly?
- What is the one biggest visual thing to fix next?
